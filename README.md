# NaijaStock Distributors Ltd – Sales & Distribution Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It focuses on analyzing the sales and distribution performance of NaijaStock Distributors Ltd, a Nigerian distribution company dealing in packaged foods, beverages, and household products.

The goal of the project was to transform raw sales transaction data into meaningful business insights that enable management to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, evaluate profitability, and make informed strategic decisions.

## Business Problem

The company relied on separate spreadsheets across its Product, Operations, and Procurement departments, making it difficult to obtain consistent business performance information.

This project addresses that challenge by developing an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates business data into a centralized reporting solution.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis generated insights including:

- Total revenue of approximately ₦276.55 million.
- Total profit of approximately ₦85.13 million.
- A total of 5,139 orders processed.
- Revenue growth of approximately 123% year over year.
- Packaged Foods emerged as the leading revenue-contributing category.
- Lagos Mainland recorded the highest warehouse performance, while Abuja recorded the lowest.
- Delta Foods emerged as the leading supplier by performance.

## Project Objectives

- Consolidate business data into a centralized reporting system.
- Identify top-performing products, warehouses, and suppliers.
- Monitor revenue and profitability trends.
- Evaluate supplier and warehouse performance.
- Provide actionable insights to support business decisions.

This project demonstrates how data analytics and business intelligence tools can transform raw business data into actionable insights. The interactive Power BI report provides management with a clearer understanding of business performance and supports informed decision-making across product management, operations, and procurement.
